Built in South Africa

An established talent market for global work.

South Africa has a mature customer experience and business services industry, with experienced talent already accustomed to supporting international companies.

Strong English-language capability, experienced operational leaders, time-zone alignment and an established service ecosystem make the market compelling.

But the strongest reason to build here is the people.

Experienced talent

A workforce that already understands global customer operations.

Global communication

Strong English-language capability and cultural familiarity with international customers.

Connected working hours

Meaningful overlap with Europe and the US.

Mature ecosystem

An established market with experienced managers, specialists and operational talent.
